Welcome to Keralam

Kerala famously known as "God's Own Country," is a tropical paradise nestled on the southwest coast of India. Framed by the lush green mountains of the Western Ghats on one side and the blue waters of the Arabian Sea on the other, the state boasts a stunning mix of landscapes. It features a long stretch of sandy beaches, mist-covered hill stations like Munnar, and dense forests filled with unique wildlife. The famous emerald backwaters—a peaceful network of interconnected rivers, lakes, and canals—wind through the region, offering a quiet escape where life moves at a gentle pace on traditional wooden houseboats"
